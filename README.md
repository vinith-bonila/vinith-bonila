## Field notes

**Numbers come from code, the model only explains them.** AutoBI computes every KPI in Python, so its dashboards can't hallucinate - and it runs fine with no AI key at all.

**Benchmark before choosing.** AutoBI left serverless after a measured 345 MB bundle hit a 250 MB cap. DocMind's retrieval is scored on recall@5, MRR, faithfulness and relevance, not vibes.

**Ship to a URL.** Every flagship above is live and clickable, not just a repo.
